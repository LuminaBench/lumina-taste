How we count an active account

An account is active in a month if at least one person in it opened a dashboard, ran a query or received an alert they then clicked. Signing in alone does not count.

We chose this because sign-ins overstate use: a third of sign-ins end within ten seconds, usually someone checking a password. Opening a dashboard is the first thing that shows intent.

The count is taken at midnight UTC on the last day of the month. Accounts created that day are included; accounts cancelled that day are not.

If your own figure differs, the usual cause is time zone. Export the list from Settings and compare the dates on the edge of the month first.
