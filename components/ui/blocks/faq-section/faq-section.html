Questions

Something else? Write to us; a person replies within a day.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual plans cost the same as ten months.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) by default, or the US (Virginia) if you choose it when you sign up.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Settings. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
