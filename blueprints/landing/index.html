Before you start

Something else? Write to us; a person replies within a day.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect a database and have their first dashboard in under an hour. We help with the first one on a call if you like.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) by default, or the US (Virginia) if you choose it when you sign up. We read from your sources and never sell or share data.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Settings. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you can export everything first.
