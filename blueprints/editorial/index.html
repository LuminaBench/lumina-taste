From the notebook

The form is the service

Most people never see the policy, the database or the case worker. They see a form. If the form asks the same question twice, the service asks it twice. If the form uses a word nobody says out loud, the service is speaking a language nobody chose.

So I start every project by reading the form aloud to someone who has to fill it in. Not a usability lab, just a kitchen table and a cup of tea. The first time someone frowns, that is where the work begins.

If the form asks the same question twice, the service asks it twice.

The changes are rarely dramatic. One question per page. Dates in three boxes, not a picker. “Your income” instead of “Gross taxable receipts”. Done together, they turn a form that half of people abandon into one almost everybody finishes.
