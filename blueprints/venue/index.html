Before you come

Anything else, call 0117 496 0123. Someone at the counter will answer.

Do I need to book a table?

No. The café is walk-in only. Tastings are the one thing to book, because each has eight places.

Can you grind the beans for me?

Yes. Tell us how you brew when you order and we grind to suit, on the day we post.

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast every Tuesday and post on Wednesday. Each bag has its roast date on the back.

Is the roastery step-free?

Yes, from the lane to the counter and the tasting room. There is an accessible toilet by the yard door.
