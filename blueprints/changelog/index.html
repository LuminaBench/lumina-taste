Changelog

What changed in Halyard

Every release, newest first. Most weeks bring one.

RSS feed · Email me when something ships

Releases

  1. 24 Nov 2026v4.2

    New Realtime alerts

    Alerts fire within 30 seconds of a threshold being crossed, instead of on the next hourly run. Set them from any chart menu.

  2. 10 Nov 2026v4.1.2

    Fixed CSV exports keep your column order

    Exports from the table view now match what is on screen, including hidden columns you turned back on.

  3. 30 Oct 2026v4.1

    Improved Shared dashboards keep their filters

    When you share a dashboard, the person you send it to sees the same date range and segments you were looking at.

  4. 14 Oct 2026v4.0.3

    Fixed Week numbers in January

    Charts grouped by week no longer show a week 53 at the start of the year.

  5. 18 Sep 2026v4.0

    New A new chart engine

    Lines end with their labels, the crosshair snaps to the nearest point and every chart works with the keyboard.

  6. 2 Sep 2026v3.9

    Improved Queries return 3× faster

    A new query planner caches the last 24 hours, so most dashboards load in under 300 ms.

  7. 12 Aug 2026v3.8

    New Comments on charts

    Pin a comment to a point on any chart. Teammates are notified and the thread stays attached when the data updates.

  8. 28 Jul 2026v3.7.1

    Fixed Dark mode in emails

    Weekly digest emails are readable in dark mode in Gmail and Outlook.
