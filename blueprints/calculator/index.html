Before you rely on this

Four things people ask about the numbers above.

Is this a mortgage offer?

No. It is an illustration that assumes one fixed rate for the whole term. A lender's offer depends on your income, your outgoings and the property.

Why is my first year mostly interest?

Interest is charged on what you still owe, and at the start you owe the most. Each payment clears a little more of the loan, so the interest share falls every year.

What does a bigger deposit change?

It lowers the loan, so you pay less interest, and it lowers the loan to value. Below 90%, 85%, 75% and 60%, lenders usually offer lower rates.

Are there other costs?

Yes: stamp duty, legal fees, a survey and sometimes a lender fee. Budget for them separately; this page only covers the monthly payment.
