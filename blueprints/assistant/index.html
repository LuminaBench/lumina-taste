Why did churn rise in October?

Read-only data

  1. Why did churn rise in October?

  2. Churn rose from 1.9% to 2.6% in October. Two things explain most of it:

    1. Annual renewals. 14 annual plans came up for renewal and 5 did not renew, against 1 in a typical month.
    2. The Starter price change. 9 Starter accounts cancelled in the week after the email went out.
    PlanCancelledLost MRR
    Starter9£351
    Team4£1,920
    Scale1£2,450

    Without the renewals, October would have been 2.0%.

    From Billing ledger and Events

  3. Write the query for the Starter cancellations.

  4. This returns every Starter account that cancelled between 1 and 31 October:

    select account_id, cancelled_at, mrr
from subscriptions
where plan = 'starter'
  and cancelled_at >= '2026-10-01'
  and cancelled_at <  '2026-11-01'
order by cancelled_at;
    From Billing ledger
