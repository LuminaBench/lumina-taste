Before you book

Anything else, write to hello@larkspurassembly.example.

Can I get a refund if I cannot come?

In full until 14 April. After that, change the name on your ticket at any time, free.

Can my employer pay by invoice?

Yes. Choose "Pay by purchase order" at checkout and we send an invoice the same day, payable within 30 days.

Will the talks be recorded?

Yes, with captions. Ticket holders get them within a week; they are public three months later.

What about food allergies?

Tell us when you book. Every dish is labelled, and there are always vegan, halal and gluten-free plates.
