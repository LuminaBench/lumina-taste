Questions people ask

Anything else, call 0113 496 0177 during a session.

Do I need a referral to get food?

No. Come to any session. We ask your postcode and how many people you are feeding, and nothing else.

Can I claim Gift Aid on my donation?

Yes, if you pay UK income tax. Tick the box when you give and we claim 25p for every £1.

Is the food safe?

Yes. Everything is within its use-by date, chilled food stays chilled from shop to shelf, and the council inspects us every year.

Do you take fresh or home-cooked food?

Fresh fruit and vegetables, yes. Home-cooked food, no: we cannot check how it was made.
