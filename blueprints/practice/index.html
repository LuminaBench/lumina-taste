Treatments and fees

What we do, and what it costs

Every price is the full price. If a plan changes once we have looked, we tell you the new figure before we start.

  • Check-up and clean

    A full examination with a dentist, then a scale and polish with a hygienist. X-rays when they are needed, at no extra cost.

    45 min£68

  • White fillings

    Tooth-coloured composite, matched to the tooth beside it. Most are done in one visit under local anaesthetic.

    30 to 60 minfrom £95

  • Children's check-ups

    For under-18s registered with a parent at the practice. First visits are short and mostly about the chair.

    20 minFree

  • Same-day emergency

    Toothache, a lost filling or a broken tooth. Call before 10:00 and we see you that day, registered or not.

    30 min£85

  • Whitening at home

    Trays made to fit your teeth and two weeks of gel, with a check at the end. We say so if it will not suit you.

    2 visits£290

Prices from 1 September 2026. The full fee list covers crowns, root canal treatment, dentures and extractions.

Your first visit

Forty-five minutes, and nothing done you have not agreed to

Nervous is normal. Tell us when you book and we add fifteen minutes so nobody is rushed.

  1. 1

    We listen first

    Ten minutes in a chair that is not the dental chair: what has brought you in, what has gone badly before, any medicines you take.

  2. 2

    We look, and show you

    A full examination with photographs on the screen beside you, so you see what the dentist sees.

  3. 3

    You get a plan in writing

    What needs doing now, what can wait, and what each part costs. You take it home and decide in your own time.

Before you call

Anything else, ring 0113 496 0142. A person answers, usually Ruth.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, adults and children. The first appointment is usually within two weeks, sooner on Wednesdays when we open late.

Do you treat NHS patients?

Children under 18, yes. Adults are seen privately, and the fee list above is everything you pay.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. Plans over £300 can be paid monthly over six months with no interest and no credit check.

What if I have to cancel?

Tell us by 16:00 the working day before and there is no charge. After that we ask for £25 towards the time.
