Nine days from a ball of clay to your door

  1. Thrown and trimmed

    450 g of Staffordshire stoneware on the wheel. A day to dry, then the foot is trimmed and the handle pulled.

  2. Fired, glazed, fired again

    A first firing to 1,000°C, a dip in the oat glaze, then eleven hours at 1,240°C. The speckle is iron in the clay.

  3. Checked and packed

    Nell checks every rim and base by hand. Seconds stay in the shop. Yours goes out in paper and card, no plastic.

Delivery and returns

Anything else, write to nell@larkspurceramics.example. Nell answers within a day.

How much is delivery, and how long does it take?

£4.50 in the UK, free over £60. Orders placed before noon are posted the same day and arrive in two working days.

What if it arrives broken?

Send a photograph and we post a replacement the next working day. You do not need to send the pieces back.

Can I return something I do not like?

Yes, within 30 days, unused. We refund the full price and email a prepaid label.

Do you post outside the UK?

To the EU, yes, for £14 with duties paid at checkout. Not further yet: too many arrive in pieces.
